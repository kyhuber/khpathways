Hello, I'm Kyle.

I founded KH Pathways to provide coaching to those who want to build their own financial confidence. I am dedicated to equipping my clients with the knowledge and tools they need to take control of their financial futures. On this journey, I help clients leverage technology and data to make the complex world of finance more accessible and manageable.

From a young age, I've had a deep passion for saving and investing. I'm driven by curiosity about how financial systems work and their potential to enhance individual and business success. This interest inspired me to pursue a Bachelor's degree with Honors in Economics from Indiana University, followed by an MBA from the University of Washington, and a Graduate Certificate in Finance Technology from the University of California, Berkeley.

Throughout my professional career, I have worked with startups, prestigious large companies, and individuals, navigating a wide range of financial challenges and opportunities. My love for teaching led me to share my knowledge at the university level, instructing economics and finance technology.

As KH Pathways continues to grow, I remain committed to exploring innovative ways to better serve clients and enhance their individual journey.
