## What the draft is trying to do

1. **Say what is distinctive.** Kyle's real differentiator is that clients end up running things themselves. The current copy never says it.
2. **Name the feeling before the solution.** The Financial Confidence page already does this. The draft sharpens it.
3. **Stay consistent with the Disclaimer.** The site says coaching is educational and not financial advice. So the draft avoids "build wealth", "guarantee", and promises of results, and describes what Kyle teaches and what the client does.
4. **Fix a mismatch.** The heading "Do You Feel Uncertain About Your Financial Future?" is followed by text about debt only.
5. **Shorter and plainer.** Same tone, fewer abstract phrases ("complex landscape", "embark on this journey").

Nothing below invents credentials, numbers, results, or testimonials.

## Financial Confidence: /financial-confidence

**Headline** (unchanged): Financial Confidence Starts Here

**Intro**
> Money stress is often less about the math and more about not knowing where you stand. I help you replace avoidance and worry with a clear picture of your finances and a plan you understand and can run yourself.

**Overcoming Personal Finance Challenges**
> Do any of these sound familiar?

1. **Dreading the login?**
   If checking your balance makes your stomach drop, you're not alone. We start by looking at the numbers together, then build a simple budgeting routine so checking in becomes ordinary.
2. **Is debt weighing on you?**
   We'll lay out what you owe, choose an order for paying it down, and set a plan you can follow. Seeing the whole picture often takes the edge off the worry.
3. **Not sure how to grow your savings?**
   Learn how saving works, set up habits that fit your income, and decide what your savings are for.
4. **Wondering if you'll ever retire?**
   Retirement planning can feel too big to start. We break it into steps, and starting early gives you more options.

*Why:* each heading now matches its paragraph (fixes the debt mismatch). Shorter headings scan better next to the numerals.

**Support for where you are** (replaces "Building a Strong Financial Foundation")
> Coaching is tailored to your circumstances.

- **Aspiring savers and investors:** Learn the basics of saving and investing so you can make informed choices of your own.
- **If financial worry keeps you up at night:** Build a clear plan and a cushion for the unexpected, so surprises feel manageable.
- **Anyone seeking financial empowerment:** Whether you're planning a major life event or just want to understand money better, I teach you what you need to know.

**One line to add near the closing button (recommended):**
